📱 Important: Getting Notifications On Your Phone
  • iPhone / iPad (iOS Safari): Apple requires web apps to be installed to the home screen for lock screen push alerts. Tap Share ⎋ at bottom of Safari → tap "Add to Home Screen" → open the app from your home screen and enable notifications.
  • Android (Chrome): Tap the 🔔 Notifications menu → tap Enable → press Allow when prompted by Chrome.
  • In-App Banners & Audio Chimes: Even without phone lock screen push alerts, in-app visual banners and sound chimes remain 100% active whenever the page is open!
